    How Radar Predictions Are Derived

    The radar algorithm computes spatial density clusters of verified civic reports within a 500m radius, weighted by category recurrence frequency, seasonal rainfall indices, and infrastructure age. Recommendations are submitted to municipal department engineers for scheduled preventative overhauls.
